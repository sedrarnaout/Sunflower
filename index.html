The Sunflower

Sunflower,(Helianthus annuus), annual flowering plant known for its spectacular size, large, showy flower heads, and edible seeds. The sunflower is native to North America and is widely cultivated as an ornamental and as a commercial food crop, its seeds being the source of sunflower oil.

  • Origin :
    North America
  • Height :
    1.5 to 3 meters
  • Season :
    Summer to Fall
